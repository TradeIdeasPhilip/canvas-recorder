Media Browser

A quick strawman for a CapCut-"Media tab"-style tool: paste video URLs, see their real duration/size/frame rate, and produce VideoClipComponent-shaped JSON to paste into your source. Cross-origin URLs need CORS headers from the host or this will fail to probe them.
